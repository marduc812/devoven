# Code of conduct

DevOven is a small project maintained by one person. This document is short
because the expectations are short.

## The standard

Be decent to people. Argue about the code, not the person writing it. Assume
the other side is acting in good faith until they give you a concrete reason to
think otherwise, and remember that most contributors here are working in a
second language and on their own time.

Not welcome, in issues, pull requests, reviews or commit messages:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning comments about who someone is: their nationality, gender, sexual
  orientation, disability, appearance, race, age, religion, or level of
  experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments, brigading a thread, or
  demanding free work from a maintainer.

Disagreement is fine and useful. A maintainer saying no to your idea is not
mistreatment.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project uses, and to anyone representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/marduc812/devoven/security/advisories/new)
if it involves anything you would rather not post in public, or open an issue
if it does not.

The maintainer will look at it and respond. Depending on what happened, that
means a warning, edited or deleted comments, or a block from the repository.
Reports are handled privately and the reporter's identity is not published.

There is no committee and no appeals process. It is one person trying to keep a
tools website pleasant to contribute to.
